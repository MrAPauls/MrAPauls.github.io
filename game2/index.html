How to Play

  • Start at the green tile.
  • On phones or tablets, tap or drag your finger across the path.
  • Flip as many tiles as possible on the path to the red tile.
  • Tiles can only be flipped horizontally or vertically.
  • Go off the path: -1 point and start again at the green tile.
